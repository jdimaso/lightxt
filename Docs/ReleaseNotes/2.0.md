# LighTxt 2.0

- **Select cells in a CSV.** Click a cell or drag across a range to see the count, sum, average, median, minimum, and maximum. Click row numbers to select whole rows. Dollar amounts, thousands separators, and accounting negatives work in calculations and numeric sorting.
- **Explore a CSV with PivotViz.** Choose dimensions and metrics in a separate window. Switch between a pivot table and the bar, line, or scatter charts available for your selection. The analysis uses the rows in your filtered CSV.
- **Read nested JSON more easily.** The light-mode tree has a white background, gray field names, and darker values. Wide windows show each field beside its value. Resizing or zooming preserves your selection and expanded branches.
- **Navigate JSON Lines by record.** Open `.jsonl` or `.ndjson`, jump to a record by number, and expand its contents. A malformed record shows its error and you can keep browsing later records.
- **Choose Parquet columns.** Search the schema and choose the fields to show in the table and include in exports.
- **Recover unsaved work.** Review recoverable edits at launch. If another app changes an open file in place, use **Export Local Edits…** to save your changes for recovery.

Version 2.0 can restore recovery journals from 1.9. If you later return to 1.9, save your open documents in 2.0 first. The newer recovery checkpoints require 2.0.
